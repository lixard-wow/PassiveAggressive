Season 2 • Returning

Temple of Sethraliss

4 Bosses • Vol'dun — A sunken loa temple where storm-blessed sethrak guardians and corrupted lightning elementals stand between you and the Heart of Sethraliss herself.

⚔ Trash Pull Notes

📶 Priority Targets
  • Agitated Nimbus — charges up a room-wide burst, don't let it sit.
  • Barbed Krolusk — stacking bleed via Serrated Charge, dangerous in packs.
  • Sandswept Hunter — ranged burst via Arrow Barrage.
⚡ Must Kick
  • Lightning Bolt (Storm Adept / Imbued Stormcaller) — kick on sight.
  • Arrow Barrage (Sandswept Hunter) — interrupt every cast.
🔒 CC Targets
  • Sandswept Hunter & Barbed Krolusk — standard CC candidates.
🔮 Purge / Soothe
  • Purge/dispel Accumulate Charge (Agitated Nimbus) before it releases as Release Charge AoE — or hold defensives if it goes off anyway.
Boss 1 of 4

Adderis and Aspix

A dual-boss guardian fight built around a Lightning Shield that swaps between the two, changing which one uses lightning attacks versus wind-based ones.

🛡 Tank
  • Track which sethrak currently holds Lightning Shield — that one hits harder and gains lightning abilities.
  • Sidestep/position for Cyclone Strike (frontal cone) and Arcing Blade (damage split among nearby players).
  • Watch energy — 50 triggers A Peal of Thunder (raid-wide burst), 100 triggers Arc Dash (teleport strike).
✚ Healer
  • Save cooldowns for the big raid-damage windows: A Peal of Thunder (50 energy) and Arc Dash (100 energy).
  • Watch for Gale Force, a channeled knockback wind that can scatter the group out of healing range.
  • Damage is bursty rather than sustained — top players before energy thresholds hit.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: Gust (Aspix's direct nature-damage bolt) whenever available to reduce chip damage.
  • Help control positioning so Arc Dash's path doesn't cross the healer.
  • Watch personal cooldown timing around the 50/100 energy bursts.
Boss 2 of 4

Merektha

A burrowing serpent boss that alternates burrow phases with an active nest, forcing the group to manage hatching snake adds while dodging ground slams.

🛡 Tank
  • Face Merektha away from the group during Serpentstorm (frontal knockback exhale).
  • Reposition immediately when she casts Burrow — she resurfaces elsewhere with AoE Burrowquake plus a knockup/stun on impact.
  • Peel or reposition adds spawned from Hatch away from healers.
✚ Healer
  • Dispel/track Thunder Spit's lingering lightning-strike DoT ticks.
  • Watch for Knot of Snakes on higher keys — a full-action-lock wrap that needs an external cooldown or stun-break, not healing alone.
  • Keep the group topped through Burrowquake's repeated ground pulses.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: Lightning Bite when it targets a non-tank, and prioritize killing hatched snake adds fast.
  • Kill Toxic Viper adds quickly — their Poison Spit stacks a nasty DoT.
  • Focus Storm Serpent adds to stop their Storm Catalyst lightning pools from covering the floor.
Boss 3 of 4

Galvazzt

An energy-management fight where players must intercept Lightning Spire beams to stop Galvazzt from charging up his room-wiping Consume Charge.

🛡 Tank
  • Tank Galvazzt near the center so ranged can rotate through Lightning Spire beams easily.
  • Watch your own Galvanized debuff uptime if soaking — it hugely increases physical damage taken.
  • Be ready to reposition off Induction pulse/field zones.
✚ Healer
  • Track his energy bar — every uninterrupted Lightning Spire feeds Consume Charge, a heavy raid-wide hit plus a stacking Capacitance buff on him.
  • Heal through Induction Field's persistent tick damage if anyone lingers in it.
  • Save a cooldown for each Consume Charge release, especially after multiple Capacitance stacks.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: nothing directly castable to interrupt — instead soak Lightning Spire beams on rotation to deny Energize stacks.
  • Get out of Induction Field zones quickly rather than eating repeated ticks.
  • Coordinate cooldowns around soak duty rather than pushing burst right after a Consume Charge.
Boss 4 of 4 — Final

Avatar of Sethraliss

An inverted boss fight — the group must heal the Avatar back to full while surviving corrupted adds and a healing-preventing Defiling Taint.

🛡 Tank
  • Group Corrupted Guardian adds for cleave, watch Tainted Strike and Vile Charge.
  • Peel Corrupted Guardians targeting healers via Shadowlash's healing-reduction fixate.
  • Position adds so Corrupted Lifeforce orbs (from Unstable Corruption) can be soaked safely.
✚ Healer
  • Core job: pump healing into the Avatar herself while managing the Corruption debuff (reduces healing done 33%, raises physical damage taken 250% on the carrier).
  • Interrupt or stop Defiling Taint from the Essence Defiler add — it blocks all external healing on the Avatar while active.
  • Manage Corruption Burst timing — cleanse or soak Corrupted Lifeforce before it detonates.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: Defiling Taint (Essence Defiler's healing-prevention channel) — top priority every cast.
  • Kill Essence Defilers and Corrupted Guardians fast to reduce total incoming Corruption stacks.
  • Control add health carefully during Siphon the Weak windows — the Avatar executes weakened Tormentors for self-healing.
