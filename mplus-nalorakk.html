Season 2 • New

Den of Nalorakk

3 Bosses • Zul'Aman — Push through a scavenger's den and a frozen sentinel's lair to face Nalorakk, the bear loa of war, in his own throne room.

⚔ Trash Pull Notes

📶 Priority Targets
  • Loa Speaker Nanea — tough caster, Volatile Totem deals raid damage. Focus first.
  • Spirit of Hunger — tough, Starvation Effigy. High priority.
  • Grizzled Warbringer — tough, applies Primal Echo. Merits early attention.
⚡ Must Kick
  • Frigid Roar (Frigid Mauler) — kick on sight.
  • Healing Breeze (Earthwhisper Tender) — priority over its secondary Earth Bolt.
  • Arc Lightning (Stormbound Mystic) — priority over Lightning Bolt.
🔒 CC Targets
  • Earthwhisper Tender & Bonded Beasttamer — standard CC. Most other named mobs in this dungeon are tagged "tough" and CC-immune.
🔮 Purge / Soothe
  • Purge/dispel Shredding Claws (Thornclaw Gatherer), Mother's Wrath (Territorial Matriarch), and Bestial Wrath (Bonded Beasttamer) tank-damage buffs.
  • Dispel Starvation Effigy where possible.
Boss 1 of 3

The Hoardmonger

A scavenger troll whose stolen meat, bone, and mushroom piles empower his kit at 90/70/40% health — centered on soaking Rotten Mushrooms before they explode.

🛡 Tank
  • Face Earthshatter Slam's frontal cone away from the group — it upgrades to the area-denial Bonespike Slam.
  • Watch for added knockback once Hearty Bellow triggers.
  • Help soak mushrooms when needed.
✚ Healer
  • Pre-cooldown for Ravenous Bellow/Hearty Bellow raid-wide roar damage.
  • Track stacking Toxic Spores poison on players who've soaked multiple mushrooms.
  • Expect a damage spike at each 90/70/40% health-threshold empowerment.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: no boss-cast interrupt is documented — it's a soak/positioning check, hold kicks for nearby trash.
  • Soak Rotten Mushrooms promptly during Spoiled Supplies.
  • Push damage before each 90/70/40% threshold to limit his upgraded abilities.
Boss 2 of 3

Sentinel of Winter

An icy, sliding arena where adds from Shattering Frostspike must be interrupted and burned down while Glacial Torment stacks on the raid.

🛡 Tank
  • Position the boss near spawned Fractured Shivercore adds for cleave.
  • Mind the slick floor and Frozen Tempest's knockback near ledges.
  • Pick up adds fast after each Shattering Frostspike.
✚ Healer
  • Dispel Glacial Torment before it stacks out of control.
  • Save cooldowns for Frozen Tempest's ticking group damage.
  • Heal through Raging Squall as the group stacks to funnel tornadoes into one path.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: Winter's Shroud, cast by the Fractured Shivercore adds spawned during Shattering Frostspike.
  • Group up to bait Raging Squall tornadoes together.
  • Soak Rimeshatter piles and kill adds quickly.
Boss 3 of 3 — Final

Nalorakk

The bear loa of war, fought while protecting ally Zul'jarra, soaking a tank buster, and dodging charging spectral echoes.

🛡 Tank
  • Soak the Forceful Slam tank buster after each Overwhelming Onslaught — blocking it prevents raid-wide Demoralizing Scream.
  • Reposition so the group can shelter behind the shield during Overwhelming Onslaught.
  • Watch positioning if hit by Echoing Maul, which spawns Spectral Slash-dealing Echoes.
✚ Healer
  • Big cooldowns for the Overwhelming Onslaught damage window.
  • Heal through follow-up Spectral Slash hits from Echoing Maul.
  • Keep Zul'jarra alive through Fury of the War God.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: no confirmed boss-cast interrupt published yet — treat this as a positioning/soak fight.
  • Form a wall to block charging Echoes during Fury of the War God.
  • Kill Echoing Maul's Echo spawns before they reach Zul'jarra.
