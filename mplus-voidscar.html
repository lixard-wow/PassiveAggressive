Season 2 • New

Voidscar Arena

3 Bosses • Northern Voidstorm • A shadow-touched gladiatorial pit run by a cosmic showman and his captive champions. A positioning and coordination check more than an interrupt check.

⚔ Trash Pull Notes

📶 Priority Targets
  • Brutal Overseer — burn its Brutal Slams shield down before it detonates for stacking group-wide damage.
  • Enthralled Shaman — kill fast and destroy its Magma Totem the instant it spawns.
  • Voidtouched Magi — high burst caster, keep interrupts rotating on it.
⚡ Must Kick
  • Shadowbolt Volley (Voidtouched Magi) — top priority.
  • Demoralizing Shout (Dominated Brawler) — raid-wide damage debuff if it lands.
  • Mad Shriek (Kilivore Screamer) — kick on sight.
  • Lava Bolt (Enthralled Shaman) — kick when interrupts are free.
🔒 CC Targets
  • Feral Saberon — leaping/mobile threat, CC on pull.
  • Protective Turtle — isolate, its Shell Guard buffs nearby allies.
  • Dominated Brawler — control while interrupts are on cooldown.
🔮 Purge / Soothe
  • Melt Armor (Sycophantic Tarasek) — dispel/cleanse the tank armor reduction.
  • Corrosive Essence stacks — poison dispel where available.
Boss 1 of 3

Taz'Rah

A void-bound arena champion fighting against his will — built around line-attack dodging and ground-pool control. No interruptible cast is documented; this fight is a positioning check.

🛡 Tank
  • Use active mitigation on Void Blast.
  • Avoid knocking yourself or the boss into an active Void Fissure.
  • Give the raid room to spread for Nether Dash's line attack, and manage adds near Umbral Rupture puddles.
✚ Healer
  • Top the group off before Umbral Rupture puddles resolve.
  • Watch for players clipped by Nether Dash's line attack.
  • Track Dark Bloom orb spread — it punishes stacking.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: no boss cast is confirmed interruptible on Taz'Rah — save interrupts for adds and trash instead.
  • Spread for Nether Dash, then regroup before Umbral Rupture.
  • Dodge orbs spawned by Dark Bloom, and help clear adds near Void Fissure zones.
Boss 2 of 3

Atroxus

A starved behemoth imprisoned by the arena's owner, cycling breath damage, poison ground effects, and a punishing add phase.

🛡 Tank
  • Face Noxious Breath's frontal away from the group.
  • Use a major defensive for Hulking Claw, the tank buster.
  • Reposition off Poison Splash ground pools, and keep Toxic Creeper adds close for cleave once tanked.
✚ Healer
  • Bring cooldowns for the Toxic Creeper add phase (Toxic Aura damage).
  • Dispel or cleanse poison effects (Corrosive Essence-type debuffs) when available.
  • Watch raid-wide damage during the Poison Splash channel, and pre-heal before Monstrous Roar triggers the add spawn.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: no boss cast confirmed interruptible on Atroxus — priority is killing Toxic Creeper adds fast after Monstrous Roar.
  • Avoid Poison Splash ground pools and sidestep Noxious Breath's frontal.
  • Burst Toxic Creepers down quickly — their Sickening Bite is lethal if left up.
Boss 3 of 3 — Final

Charonus

The arena's owner — a coordination-heavy finale built around kiting Gravitic Orbs into Unstable Singularity stars. A kiting check, not an interrupt check.

🛡 Tank
  • Use active mitigation for Dark Waves (frontal tank buster) after each orb cast.
  • Keep Charonus positioned centrally among the three singularity stars.
  • Give orb carriers room to route safely, and avoid the frontal Void Cascade projectile line.
✚ Healer
  • Prioritize healing Gravitic Orb carriers — the stacking damage ramps fast.
  • Track raid-wide damage from Cosmic Crash.
  • Keep a healing buffer up for players resolving orbs into singularities.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: no boss cast confirmed interruptible on Charonus — this fight is a positioning/kiting check.
  • If targeted by Gravitic Orbs, drag the orb into an Unstable Singularity star to destroy it — don't stand in the star yourself.
  • Spread to avoid Cosmic Crash overlap, and keep moving during Void Cascade.
