Season 2 • New

Altar of Fangs

3 Bosses • The Coiled Isle — Vaults of Atal'Utek — A cursed Amani temple where a starving hydra, a cannibal serpent brood, and a troll ritualist guard the Fang of Ula'tek. They've been devouring each other to get stronger.

⚔ Trash Pull Notes

📶 Priority Targets
  • Ascendant Serpent — keeps summoning Hatchlings and can snowball. Kill on sight.
  • Ritual Chieftain — Blood Sacrifice strains healers; destroy its Unstable Totem fast.
⚡ Must Kick
  • Piercing Hiss (Primal Serpent) — kick on sight.
  • Mass Envenom (High Evolutionist / Ula'tek's Chosen) — top priority interrupt.
🔒 CC Targets
  • Ravenous Descendant — stun to reset its stacking attack-speed buff, never pull two together.
  • Rattling Writhe — its Rattle hits hard, CC or defensive it.
🔮 Purge / Soothe
  • Paralyzing Shots (Twinfang Harrower) — dispel/purge priority.
  • Mass Envenom — also worth stripping as a poison/magic dispel if it lands.
Boss 1 of 3

Rav'i

A ravenous hydra that gorges on corpse piles around its lair, growing a shield you must burst before it can heal off the feast.

🛡 Tank
  • Keep Rav'i away from Fresh Meat piles — eating one grants Scent of Blood/Feeding Frenzy, an attack-speed buff.
  • Active-mitigate Hydrastrike.
  • Face it away from the group for its Regurgitate frontal.
✚ Healer
  • Save cooldowns for the burst right after its Ssscavenging shield pops.
  • Raid cooldown for Ravenous Stomp.
  • Extra healing if it gets fed and gains Feeding Frenzy.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: no confirmed hard interrupt for this boss — it's a shield-burst/positioning check, not a kick target.
  • Burst the Ssscavenging shield fast, and spread for Triple Shot.
  • Help deny or soak meat piles before they're eaten.
Boss 2 of 3

The Writhing Coil

A serpent brood-champion that splits into individual snakes mid-fight and punishes missed interrupts with an escalating raid-wide poison DoT.

🛡 Tank
  • Face away from the group for Tail Scythe, the physical tank buster.
  • Position for Burrowing Charge into Venom Jet.
  • Help tank grouped snakes during the Uncoil split.
✚ Healer
  • Plan cooldowns around Synchronized Venom's persistent group DoT rather than reacting to it.
  • Ramp healing during the Uncoil add phase.
  • Death Rattle is mostly a movement/mechanic check, not pure healing.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: Toxic Atrophy — must be interrupted on rotation (it recasts quickly, so split across 2–3 interrupters) or it stacks a damage-taken debuff.
  • Burst split snakes fast before Assimilation reforms them.
  • Watch for Spiteful Hunt fixates.
Boss 3 of 3 — Final

Zul'jan

The final Amani ritualist channels beams from the Fang of Ula'tek that must be soaked, then cleansed before the resulting poison stacks turn lethal.

🛡 Tank
  • Active-mitigate Chop Down, a recurring tank buster.
  • Reposition the boss during Ritual of the Fang so beam soaks and Bloodletting pools don't block the party.
  • Watch the sweeping Axegrinder.
✚ Healer
  • Pre-plan a cooldown for every Ritual of the Fang channel.
  • Spot-heal soakers clearing Ritual Venom stacks.
  • Track stack counts — a fully-stacked debuff is lethal if not cleared.
⚔ DPS
  • Hold soak assignments — all 4 beams during Ritual of the Fang must be intercepted or unsoaked beams hit the group hard.
  • Clear Ritual Venom by soaking the Boneslicer frontal.
  • Kick: no confirmed interrupt listed for Zul'jan — his check is beam-soak/stack-clear, not a kick.
