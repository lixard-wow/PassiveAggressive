Season 2 • New

The Blinding Vale

4 Bosses • Southwestern Harandar — A radiant jungle sanctum where the Light itself has been twisted into a blooming, thorn-choked predator.

⚔ Trash Pull Notes

📶 Priority Targets
  • Radiant Spellsower — its Light Bolt Volley wakes Dormant Lashers if left alone. Kill first.
  • Lightgorged Lasher — shield-break priority.
  • Sporeblight Belcher — dangerous stacking AoE, don't let the group cluster near it.
⚡ Must Kick
  • Light Bolt Volley / Light Bolt (Radiant Spellsower) — top priority.
  • Disorienting Screech (Lightfeather Petalwing) — kick on sight.
  • Seed Shot / Lightspore Shot (Leafy Grovecrawler) — interrupt every cast.
🔒 CC Targets
  • Leafy Grovecrawler & Lightfeather Petalwing — standard CC. Several lieutenants (Luminous Thornmaw, Virid Grovekeeper, Sporeblight Belcher, Potatoad Matriarch, Overgrown Hydra, Spineshield Beetle) are CC-immune.
🔮 Purge / Soothe
  • Dispel/cleanse Toxic Spew (Potatoad Matriarch) and bleeds like Thornblade (Underbrush Stalker) or Grievous Gash (Luminous Thornmaw).
  • Freedom-type effects break Bloodthorn Roots.
Boss 1 of 4

Lightblossom Trinity

A shared-health council of three linked bosses (Meittik, Lekshi, Kezkitt), forcing simultaneous soak-circle management alongside cleave tanking.

🛡 Tank
  • Stack all three near center for cleave.
  • Use active mitigation on Bedrock Slam.
  • Reposition off Fertile Loam circles once they turn hazardous, watching Thornblade cleave positioning.
✚ Healer
  • Save cooldowns for the Lightblossom Beam soak window.
  • Heal through Thornblade bleed.
  • Treat a failed soak as a wipe-level raid-damage spike.
⚔ DPS
  • Rotate 3-player soaks on the beam circles.
  • Move off Lekshi if marked by Thornblade.
  • Kick: Kezkitt's Light Bolt — a random-target cast needing a constant interrupt rotation.
Boss 2 of 4

Ikuzz the Light Hunter

A fixate-and-kite predator whose Bloodthorn Roots punish standing still. A control/kite check, not a kick check.

🛡 Tank
  • Hold defensives for the sub-50% Frenzy.
  • Position with root placement in mind, not a tank-and-spank fight.
✚ Healer
  • Bank cooldowns for Thorncaller Roar's rolling party damage, especially into the Frenzy phase.
⚔ DPS
  • AoE down Bloodthorn Roots to free rooted allies.
  • If fixated by Bloodthirsty Gaze, kite over the roots to destroy them; pre-position for Verdant Stomp knockback.
  • Kick: no clean single-target interrupt is documented for Ikuzz — this is a control/kite check.
Boss 3 of 4

Lightwarden Ruia

A druid-like custodian cycling forms — Moonkin, Bear, then Haranir — each demanding a different defensive skill.

🛡 Tank
  • Interrupt Warden's Wrath in Moonkin phase.
  • Manage stacking bleed from Grievous Thrash/Mangling Claws in Bear phase with self-healing or cleanse.
✚ Healer
  • Dodge Lightfall ground circles.
  • Top players hit by Lightfire before their tornado spawns.
  • In Bear phase, keep the Grievous Thrash target topped continuously.
⚔ DPS
  • Group up when Lightfire-debuffed to drop tornadoes together.
  • Stand alone, away from allies, if hit by Pulverizing Strikes cone.
  • Kick: Warden's Wrath (Moonkin phase cast). In the final Haranir phase, all prior mechanics repeat on an ~8-second cycle.
Boss 4 of 4 — Final

Ziekket

An add-and-orb finale where killed adds must be beamed by the boss to stop them from respawning.

🛡 Tank
  • Mitigate Thornspike's hit-plus-knockback.
  • Herd Lasher adds and line up Ziekket's frontal beam over their corpses near a platform edge.
✚ Healer
  • Manage rolling Oozing Xylem party damage throughout.
  • Give focused healing to whoever is holding high Lightbloom's Essence stacks.
⚔ DPS
  • AoE the six summoned Lashers.
  • Split orb soaks evenly rather than hoarding stacks — damage taken scales with stack count.
  • Kick: Lightspore Shot, cast by Lasher adds — interrupt or CC during the add wave. Steer the boss so its beam hits dead corpses to permanently destroy them.
