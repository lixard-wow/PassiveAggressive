Season 2 • New

Murder Row

4 Bosses • Silvermoon City • A fel-smuggling operation run by the warlock Lithiel Cinderfury. Fel-casting trash packs, a barrel-cover boss, an edge-kiting tank fight, and a gateway-timing finale. The 34-minute timer is the longest in the pool, but it is tight if you lose time on trash.

Guide content updated September 30, 2026. Balance changes may affect these notes.

More resources: Method guide • Wowhead overview

⚔ Trash Pull Notes

📶 Priority Targets
  • Bribed Captain — CC immune and buffs its allies, so kill it first in any pack. It also stacks a debuff on the tank and has a glaive frontal.
  • Shivan Punisher — CC immune. At 50% it gains Demonic Frenzy (heavy pulsing damage), so focus it down quickly once it drops. Melee should step out of Whirlwind.
  • Corrupted Warlock — CC immune. Curse of Doom lands on a random player: dispel it, or stack in it as a group. Drain Life also targets a random player. It gains a shield at 50% from Dark Pact, and if it is ignored it summons a Doomguard on death.
  • Felmaster Lucsei — CC immune mini-boss. Blade Dance stacks a DoT on the group (healers use raid cooldowns), and the tank points the Eye Beam frontal away from the group.
  • Defiled Golem — CC immune, before the final boss. Defiled Slam is a heavy channel hitting healers; avoid the Fel Beam lines and puddles.
  • Massive Felwyrm — sustained AoE damage and adds. Hold defensives and burn it quickly.
⚡ Must Kick
  • Seduction (Seductive Sayaad) — channels into a random player. Interrupt or CC it, or someone gets charmed.
  • Fel Missiles (Felonious Mage) — interrupt or CC the channel. Assign this one before the pull.
  • Scathing Review (Influential Reviewer) — interrupt it so your star rating does not drop.
  • Felfire Burst (Unleashed Imp) — hits random players. Use spare interrupts on it.
  • Fel Rage (Wrathguard Flayer) — interrupt it, or Soothe the enrage.
  • Health Funnel (Fel Invoker) — interrupt or CC it. Avoid the Felfire Bombardment circles.
  • Doom Bolt (Doomguard) — CC immune, so interrupt it.
🔒 Dispels & Defensives
  • Flay (Wrathguard Flayer) and Sharp Nail (Warehouse Worker) — random-target bleeds. Use a defensive or a bleed cleanse.
  • Curse of Doom (Corrupted Warlock) — dispel it immediately, or heal through it.
  • Drain Life (Corrupted Warlock) — random target. Use a defensive or break line of sight.
  • Shadow Bite (Trained Felhunter) — tanks watch for the buff enhancement.
🔮 Purge / Soothe
  • Back To Work! (Keen Taskmaster) — enrages the nearby Warehouse Workers. Soothe it, or kill the Taskmaster first.
  • Fel Rage (Wrathguard Flayer) — Soothe removes the enrage if you miss the interrupt.
Event • Dungeon Start and Between Bosses 1–2

Snitches, Fel Crystals & the Bar Event

Two non-boss mechanics worth planning for. Interact with the four Snitches at the start to progress. In the bar, the group takes random roles to earn a 5-star review buff.

🛡 Tank
  • Interact with the four Snitches at the start of the dungeon to progress.
  • Crystalline Fel can be picked up for single-use buffs from vendors, one per player.
✚ Healer
  • Clock In bar event: the group performs random roles (Server, Entertainer, Cleaner, Bouncer) to earn a 5-star review.
  • The 5-star review gives +10% damage and healing for 5 minutes, so it is worth doing cleanly.
⚔ DPS
  • Interrupt Scathing Review from Influential Reviewers so the star rating does not drop.
  • Grab your own Crystalline Fel buff while you are collecting.
Boss 1 of 4

Kystia Manaheart

Kill her pet Nibbles, not Kystia. Kystia's Felshield stacks make her a bad target. At 20% Nibbles turns friendly and channels Destabilized, a heavy group-damage window.

🛡 Tank
  • Face Nibbles away from the group — Fel Spray is a rotating frontal.
  • Chaos Barrage targets you and then jumps to other players. Use a defensive, especially while Mirror Images are active.
  • Step out of Fel Nova circles.
✚ Healer
  • Dispel Corroding Spittle as soon as it lands. It can take a large chunk of someone's health if it ticks.
  • Plan cooldowns for Destabilized at Nibbles' 20%.
  • Stay out of Fel Spray and Fel Nova while you heal.
⚔ DPS
  • Focus Nibbles, not Kystia.
  • Kick / CC: destroy the 5 Mirror Images when she spawns them. Assign interrupts and stuns before the pull.
  • Burst Nibbles through Destabilized, and use a defensive if Corroding Spittle lands during Mirror Images.
Boss 2 of 4

Zaen Bladesorrow

Freight crates are your cover. Each player hides behind their own barrel for Murder In A Row. Handle Fire Bomb away from the crates, and break the green Fel-Infused Freight before it spikes the group.

🛡 Tank
  • Envenom puts a poison on you. Use a defensive, and get it dispelled right away if a poison dispel is available.
  • Avoid the barrels that Same-Day Delivery spawns.
  • Keep the boss positioned so the crates stay usable as cover.
✚ Healer
  • Killing Spree is a heavy AoE channel. Have cooldowns ready, especially if Fel-Infused Freight is still up.
  • Dispel Envenom promptly, since it cuts the tank's health.
  • Find your own barrel for Murder In A Row. Do not share one.
⚔ DPS
  • Each player hides behind a separate barrel for Murder In A Row.
  • If you get Fire Bomb, take it away from the crates so it does not destroy your cover. Cleave Fel-Infused Freight to remove its group damage.
  • Do not stand in Same-Day Delivery barrels.
Boss 3 of 4

Xathuux the Annihilator

The tank kites him slowly along the room's edge, and everyone kills the Legion Axe immediately. The section before this boss is the most dangerous trash in the dungeon.

🛡 Tank
  • Legion Strike is a frontal that leaves a healing reduction. Face him away from the group and use a defensive.
  • Demonic Rage: slowly kite him along the edge of the room, using less than half the room per cast, so the puddles stay out of the way.
  • Infernal Crush is a targeted hit — use a defensive if you are picked.
✚ Healer
  • The Legion Strike healing reduction means the tank needs big healing right after it lands.
  • Axe Toss spawns a Legion Axe that deals increasing AoE damage — keep everyone topped while it is up.
  • Stay loosely spread for Infernal Crush.
⚔ DPS
  • Swap to the Legion Axe immediately — it is the top-priority kill. If you are targeted by Axe Toss, spawn it inside cleave range.
  • Loosely spread for Infernal Crush so you do not cleave allies.
  • Save cooldowns for the damage amp that comes with Demonic Rage.
Boss 4 of 4 — Final

Lithiel Cinderfury

The hardest fight in the dungeon. Searing Fel Flame pulses on the group the whole fight, Chaos Bolt needs a steady interrupt rotation, and Demonic Gateway has to be timed around Malefic Wave.

🛡 Tank
  • Her Infernal cannot be killed and fixates on the tank until the boss dies. Touching it is lethal, so keep moving the boss away from it.
  • Pick up the Summon Vilefiend add when it spawns.
  • Do not take Demonic Gateway until the Malefic Wave finishes casting and a safe side is available.
✚ Healer
  • Searing Fel Flame deals constant pulsing group damage throughout the fight — budget mana and cooldowns for the long haul.
  • Malefic Wave is lethal AoE with a fire vulnerability, so make sure the group takes the gateway only when it is safe.
  • Fingers of Gul'dan circles overlap by design; keep the group healed through the Wild Imp spawns.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: Chaos Bolt hits a random target. Keep a set interrupt rotation.
  • Fingers of Gul'dan spawns Wild Imps that cast Felfire Burst — use spare interrupts, CC, or AoE on them.
  • Wait for Malefic Wave to finish casting before using Demonic Gateway, and clear the adds first. Adds that reach the wave gain Malefic Empowerment.
