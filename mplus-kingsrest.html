Season 2 • Returning

Kings' Rest

4 Bosses • Zuldazar, Zandalar • The burial tombs of every Zandalari king who ever ruled — ending with the resurrected First King himself.

⚔ Trash Pull Notes

📶 Priority Targets
  • Seneschal M'bara — a healer-type caster keeping the pack topped. Kill first to stop the sustain race.
  • Risen Hexer — hex/curse caster that chains crowd control onto the party. High priority alongside M'bara.
  • Guard Captain Atu — buffs nearby allies with a defensive shield. Kill next once the casters are down.
⚡ Must Kick
  • Unholy Mending (Seneschal M'bara) — undoes pull progress if left uninterrupted. Top priority every cast.
  • Hex Volley (Risen Hexer) — chains a hex effect across the party. Kick every cast.
  • Hex (Phantom Hex Priest) — single-target polymorph-style CC on a random player. Interrupt on sight.
🔒 CC Targets
  • Phantom Hex Priest & Risen Hexer — standard caster/humanoid CC, Polymorph or Hex both work.
  • Animated Guardian and Shadow-Borne Champion are CC-immune — don't waste cooldowns on them, just kill or kite.
🔮 Purge / Soothe
  • Captain's Bulwark (Guard Captain Atu buff) — Purge or Spellsteal it off before it stacks.
  • Minion of Zul shields — strip with Purge/Devour Magic to speed the kill.
  • Shadow of Zul debuff effects are dispellable — clear them off affected players promptly.
Boss 1 of 4

The Golden Serpent

A molten-gold guardian. Spit Gold puddles hatch into Animated Gold adds if left to sit — clear them fast or they overwhelm the room.

🛡 Tank
  • Track cooldowns for Tail Thrash — a steady tank buster, not lethal alone but adds up over the pull.
  • Keep the boss away from Spit Gold puddles so Animated Gold adds don't spawn on top of the group.
  • Hold major cooldowns for Luster — the boss shields itself and gains +50% damage until the shield drops.
✚ Healer
  • Pre-cooldown for Serpentine Gust — a channeled AoE that also knocks players back.
  • Top the two players hit by Spit Gold before the puddle spreads and hatches.
  • Hold burst healing in reserve for when Animated Gold adds reach the group during Lucre's Call.
⚔ DPS
  • Burn down Animated Gold adds immediately — slow or CC them to buy cleave time if they spawn in numbers.
  • Kick: Serpentine Gust is an unkickable channel — use movement and defensives instead of an interrupt to get through it.
  • Save cooldowns for after Luster drops — the +50% damage shield makes burn windows before that wasteful.
Boss 2 of 4

Mchimba the Embalmer

A death-priest whose Entomb mechanic seals a teammate away — find and free them quickly while managing add cleave and fire zones.

🛡 Tank
  • Stack Half-Finished Mummy adds on the boss the moment they spawn for cleave.
  • Hold boss position so Burning Ground puddles don't cut off group positioning.
  • Watch the Drain Fluids target — be ready to peel or use a defensive to support them.
✚ Healer
  • Focus-heal whoever is targeted by Drain Fluids — it's a self-damaging channel on that player.
  • The Entombed player takes no damage while sealed — keep healing focused on the rest of the group during that window.
  • Keep the group topped through Half-Finished Mummy cleave damage.
⚔ DPS
  • Spread out for Burning Ground — don't stand in the fire puddles.
  • Free the Entombed teammate quickly — every second they're sealed is lost DPS and more add pressure on the group.
  • Kick: Wretched Discharge from Half-Finished Mummy adds — interrupt on cast.
Boss 3 of 4

The Council of Tribes

A gauntlet of Zandalari king and queen spirits fought together, capped by an elemental totem-destruction phase. Season 2 fixed the encounter order — it's no longer randomized.

🛡 Tank
  • Position to avoid Debilitating Backhand — a knockback plus debuff.
  • Keep the fight away from patrolling Whirling Axes.
  • Line up adds/totems for cleave during Call the Elements.
✚ Healer
  • Hold a big cooldown for every Severing Axe cast.
  • Spot-heal the bleed applied by Whirling Axes.
  • Sustain the group through ongoing Arc Lightning damage.
⚔ DPS
  • Stack together for the Barrel Through soak mechanic.
  • During Call the Elements, destroy totems in priority order: Explosive → Thundering → Torrent.
  • Kick: Poison Nova — every single cast. A missed interrupt here wipes the group.
Boss 4 of 4 — Final

Dazar, The First King

A two-phase fight against the resurrected king — first on foot, then mounted on his raptor for a faster, higher-damage finale once either side drops to roughly 40–50%.

🛡 Tank
  • Mitigate Blade Combo — a ramping tank-buster channel. Use a defensive early in the channel, not after.
  • Coordinate cooldown timing for Savage Maul.
  • Sidestep Impaling Spear and Quaking Leap — both punish standing still.
✚ Healer
  • Save major cooldowns for Gilded Destruction — the fight's highest-damage moment.
  • Heal through Aerial Smash spread damage — hits 2 players on foot, expands to 4 once mounted.
  • Expect a jump in healing demand once the mount phase begins around 80%.
⚔ DPS
  • Spread for Aerial Smash to avoid overlapping the splash damage.
  • Keep the boss angled away from the group during Gilded Destruction.
  • Kick: Deathly Roar, cast by the summoned ally — interrupt every cast.
