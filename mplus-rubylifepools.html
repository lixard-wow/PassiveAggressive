Season 2 • Returning

Ruby Life Pools

3 Bosses • Waking Shores, Dragon Isles • A fast interrupt-heavy sprint through a red dragonflight sanctuary where frost and fire rituals collide before a dragon-and-rider finale.

⚔ Trash Pull Notes

📶 Priority Targets
  • Primal Thundercloud — purge its shield before it detonates, then kill first.
  • Flamegullet packs — Molten Blood burn ramps hard below 50% health, don't let them linger.
  • High Channeler Ryvati — heavy caster support for her pack, kill on priority.
⚡ Must Kick
  • Frostbolt / Ice Shield (Flashfrost Chillweaver) — kick on sight.
  • Cinderbolt (Primalist Cinderweaver) — kick every cast.
  • Thunder Blast / Flashfire (Tempest/Flame Channeler) — high priority interrupt.
  • Shock Blast (High Channeler Ryvati) — kick before it lands.
🔒 CC Targets
  • Flashfrost Chillweaver & Primalist Cinderweaver — squishy casters, CC on pull.
  • Avoid disturbing unhatched dragon eggs — triggering them early spawns Infused Whelps.
🔮 Purge / Soothe
  • Stormcloud / Tempest Barrier (Primal Thundercloud) — purge before it detonates.
  • Tempest Stormshield (High Channeler Ryvati) — purge or dispel to speed the kill.
Boss 1 of 3

Melidrussa Chillworn

A frost ritualist who shields herself in ice and floods the room with whelps and hazardous frost pools. Season 2 made her Frigid Shard interruptible — use it.

🛡 Tank
  • Pull Infused Whelps onto the boss for cleave the moment they spawn.
  • Hold boss position to keep Hailbomb/Hailburst ground effects away from the group.
  • Reposition after Chillstorm's knockback — softened in Season 2, but still worth tracking.
✚ Healer
  • Save cooldowns for periodic Chillstorm burst damage.
  • Track Frost Overload's channeled pulsing damage — be ready to top the group as it's broken.
  • Top players hit by Hailbomb ground explosions.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: Frigid Shard — now interruptible as of Season 2, assign it every cast, don't skip it.
  • Burst down Infused Whelps immediately when they spawn.
  • Help break/interrupt the Frost Overload channel.
Boss 2 of 3

Kokia Blazehoof

A fire ritual boss whose Blazebound Firestorm adds and tank-shredding Searing Blows demand tight interrupt and cooldown discipline.

🛡 Tank
  • Use a defensive on every Searing Blows tank buster — it stacks a Searing Wounds bleed.
  • Position the boss so Blazebound Firestorm adds can be cleaved.
  • Dodge or bait Molten Boulder away from the group — it stuns on hit.
✚ Healer
  • Prepare for unavoidable Inferno AoE damage from Blazebound Firestorm adds.
  • Heal through the Ritual of Blazebinding target's damage.
  • Track Searing Wounds stacks on the tank for extra healing.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: Roaring Blaze, cast by the Blazebound Firestorm add — interrupt or avoid it.
  • Focus Blazebound Firestorm adds the moment they spawn from Ritual of Blazebinding.
  • Bait or avoid Molten Boulder projectiles — step out of Scorched Earth patches.
Boss 3 of 3 — Final

Kyrakka and Erkhart Stormvein

A two-phase dragon-and-rider gauntlet. Erkhart tanks alone at first, then mounts the airborne Kyrakka around 40–50% for a faster, higher-damage finale.

🛡 Tank
  • In Phase 1, manage Stormslam — a stacking dispellable debuff raising Nature damage taken 100% per stack.
  • Reposition for Roaring Firebreath frontal cleave uptime without hitting the raid.
  • Brace for the Phase 2 merge (Erkhart mounts Kyrakka) — sustained damage rises sharply.
✚ Healer
  • Dispel Stormslam off the tank after each stack, before it reapplies.
  • Heal Inferno Spit targets — hits 2 players in Phase 1, expands to 3 in Phase 2.
  • Save cooldowns for the Phase 2 merge, when damage intensifies.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: Coordinate interrupts on Erkhart's casts — don't cast through the Cloudburst lockout window.
  • Maximize damage on Kyrakka while grounded and vulnerable in Phase 1, before the merge.
  • Avoid standing in front of Roaring Firebreath, and dodge Winds of Change knockback.
