Season 2 • Current Tier

The Venomous Abyss

8 Bosses • Opened August 18, 2026 • Mythic unlocked same week • Sunk beneath the Coiled Isle inside the venom-flooded Vaults of Atal'Utek, where the Amani's ancient pact with the corrupted serpent-goddess Ula'tek finally comes due.

Boss 1 of 8

Nek'zali the Soulcoiler

A soul-devouring well-guardian whose energy bar is a soft enrage timer — feed it too many spirits and she goes berserk.

🛡 Tank
  • Coordinate swaps around 5–6 stacks of Hollowing Strikes.
  • Manage tanking Possession Barrage.
  • Keep Nek'zali positioned away from the well and the raid.
✚ Healer
  • Prepare burst windows for Soulcoil Rite (raid-wide Shadow damage).
  • Sustain the group through Uncoiled Rage.
  • Track Essence Rend's healing-reduction debuff.
⚔ DPS
  • Kill priority on Latent Cultists and Raised Amani adds.
  • Ranged switch fast to Echoes of Jawae during the intermission.
  • Kick: melee watch well positioning to avoid overlap during add cleave.
🛡 Tank
  • Possession Barrage now applies a stacking tank debuff — rotate defensives more aggressively than Normal.
  • Corpses left by dead adds inflict Corpse Blight — keep the boss clear of them.
✚ Healer
  • Soulcoil Ignition adds an extra Ritual Burn stack — healing demand ramps faster than Normal.
  • Watch for players caught in Corpse Blight zones and spot-heal through it.
⚔ DPS
  • Spread out to avoid overlapping Corpse Blight from dead adds.
  • Kill adds fast — Heroic's extra Ritual Burn stack punishes slow add clears more than Normal.
🛡 Tank
  • Grasping Depths pulls the whole raid toward the well — hold position to help anchor the group.
  • Watch for Invoke's silence effect landing on interrupt-rotation players.
✚ Healer
  • Top the rotating dive squad before they jump in against Grasping Depths — they'll be isolated from the main healing group briefly.
  • Keep the anchored raid topped against the well's stacking pull damage.
⚔ DPS
  • Split into three squads (roughly 4 DPS + 1 healer each) and rotate diving in to kill a Drowned Echo before the raid takes lethal stacking damage from Grasping Depths.
  • This was the first boss to fall in the Race to World First — well documented, execution-heavy rather than a raw DPS check.
Boss 2 of 8

Entombed Sentinels

Twin venom golems fought by two split raid groups 40+ yards apart — get too close and they shield each other's damage by 99%.

🛡 Tank
  • Split-tank each Sentinel separately, 40+ yards apart.
  • Swap tanking assignment after every intermission so mark stacks fall off.
✚ Healer
  • Track Blood Venom dispels — cleanse only at the room's edge so pools don't clutter the arena.
  • Support the mandatory group soak on Unstable Miasma.
⚔ DPS
  • Destroy Toxic Droplets by stepping on them before they erupt.
  • Help soak Unstable Miasma as a group within 8 yards.
  • Kick: kill priority is whichever add group is closer to bursting its droplets, no confirmed boss interrupt.

Boss 4 of 8

Vashnik the Malignant

A venom fountain fight where the boss must be walked between altars so no single element ever stacks out of control.

🛡 Tank
  • Physically drag Vashnik between fountains so Imbibe never doubles up the same element twice in a row (e.g. Shadow+Flame → Flame+Blood → Blood+Shadow).
✚ Healer
  • Brace for raid damage on every Imbibe cast.
  • Watch environmental venom stacks — they never decay and drive the eventual enrage.
⚔ DPS
  • Kill priority: Burning Venoms (Fire) first, then Clotting Venom (Blood, CC-immune, splits on death — kill every piece), then Shrouded Venoms (Shadow, CC-able).
  • Keep all adds from reaching the central Malignant Cavity.

  • Not yet documented in detail — same core mechanics as Normal, expect a stricter fountain rotation.
✚ Healer
  • Not yet documented in detail — expect faster venom stack accumulation than Normal.

  • Not yet documented in detail — kill priority order likely unchanged, tighter timing.

Boss 5 of 8

Sszorak

A wind-tunnel arena and a brutal tank-swap combo turn this into a physical-damage attrition fight for healers.

🛡 Tank
  • Pass the boss between two tanks mid-combo during the Apex Predator sequence — taunt works mid-cast.
  • Point Ravage's frontal cone away from the raid.
✚ Healer
  • Treat the fight as sustained attrition with repeated bursts rather than one big cooldown moment.
  • A 5th healer is a viable option given the incoming damage.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: two full soak groups are needed for Mutilate (500% damage-taken debuff if unsoaked) — treat it as the fight's real interrupt-equivalent check.
  • Drop Viscous Cysts from Venomous Surge at assigned markers, one per marker max.
🛡 Tank
  • Not yet documented in detail — same core mechanics as Normal, expect a faster Apex Predator swap cadence.
✚ Healer
  • Not yet documented in detail — the attrition curve is steeper than Normal, plan cooldowns earlier.
⚔ DPS
  • Not yet documented in detail — Mutilate soak groups likely need to be larger or rotate faster.

Boss 6 of 8

The Twin Fangs

Two serpents, one shared debuff economy — Eternal Venom stacks kill at 10, and there's exactly one way to shed them.

🛡 Tank
  • Vexhul's tank takes a DoT that spreads Caustic Globules nearby.
  • Both serpents must die together — the survivor snowballs via Uncoiled Wrath.
✚ Healer
  • Track Eternal Venom stacking raid-wide — only Ravenous Feast at the Submerge intermission removes stacks (1 per player).
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: soak Caustic Globules quickly (run over them) before their 10-second timer explodes and applies unavoidable venom to everyone.
  • Manage the venom economy carefully before spending stacks at Feast.
🛡 Tank
  • Not yet documented in detail — same core mechanics as Normal, expect a faster venom stack economy.
✚ Healer
  • Not yet documented in detail — the 10-stack death threshold is unforgiving, plan Feast timing carefully.

Boss 7 of 8

The Coiled Altar — Zul'jan & Hex Lord Malacrass

The penultimate boss: Zul'jan's poison assault, Malacrass's possession phase, then a soulbound two-boss burn where letting one die early buffs the other.

🛡 Tank
  • Handle Zul'jan's Guillotine/Sever axe throws in Phase 1.
  • In Phase 3, keep both bosses controlled — they're Soulbound, so one death buffs the other's damage and speed.
✚ Healer
  • Heal through Venomfang's rotating poison DoT and sustained Nature damage in Phase 1.
  • Manage Malacrass's possessed players marching toward the edge in Phase 2.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: priority on Malacrass's Dreadmarch/Soul Sever.
  • In Phase 3, bring both bosses down as close to simultaneously as possible to avoid the Soulbound damage spike.

  • Not yet documented in detail — same core mechanics as Normal. This boss required 310 pulls for the eventual World First Mythic kill by Liquid, giving a sense of its difficulty ceiling.

  • Not yet documented in detail — expect a much tighter Phase 3 Soulbound execution window than Normal.

Boss 8 of 8 — Final

Ula'tek

The final boss — a three-phase venom-goddess fight built on egg control, target priority, and raid-wide coordination.

🛡 Tank
  • Take Mother's Wrath personally — move into the resulting red circle to eat the follow-up hit away from the raid.
  • Manage Blightscale Spawn positioning near Caustic Waves.
✚ Healer
  • Track Petrifying Sting's massive absorb-eating Nature DoT on affected players.
  • Heal through the raid-wide hit from Spectral Coils — mitigated by more players soaking.
⚔ DPS
  • Kick: kill Blightscale Spawn before they touch Ula'tek's venom and hatch into Putrid Membrane carriers.
  • Soak Spectral Coils to reduce the raid-wide hit.
🛡 Tank
  • Not yet documented in detail — same core mechanics as Normal, tuned tighter. A world-first Heroic kill has already been reported.
✚ Healer
  • Not yet documented in detail — expect faster Petrifying Sting stacking than Normal.
⚔ DPS
  • Not yet documented in detail — Blightscale Spawn kill windows are likely shorter than Normal.
🛡 Tank
  • Confirmed killed for World First by Liquid — full ability-by-ability Mythic breakdown still emerging as more guilds progress. Check Wowhead/Method for the latest.
✚ Healer
  • Mythic-specific healing breakdown not yet fully published — check Wowhead/Method for updates.
⚔ DPS
  • Key documented Mythic change: eggs spawn shielded and must be broken open before they can be picked up, and players carrying eggs who bump into one another cause them to hatch (likely a wipe) — the rest of the ability breakdown is still emerging, check Wowhead/Method for updates.
